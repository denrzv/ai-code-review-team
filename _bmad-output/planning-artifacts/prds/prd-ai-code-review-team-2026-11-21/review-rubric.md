# PRD Quality Review — AI Code Review Team

## Overall verdict
This is a strong PRD for a solo learning PoC. It has a real thesis ("does a planned multi-agent team find more real defects than a single agent, for comparable cost?"), counter-metrics, explicit non-goals and a complete Assumptions Index, and it fits its single-operator shape without filler. The risk sits in a few undefined terms that the headline experiment and the state machine depend on: "comparable total budget", "coverage", the precision/recall matching rule (left as OQ-8), and a glossary definition of PARTIAL that contradicts the CANCELLED/FAILED states. Until these are pinned down, SM-1 cannot be computed the same way twice, and story writers will have to guess at terminal-state behaviour.

## Decision-readiness — adequate
Most choices are stated as decisions, not "considerations". Examples: "'Multi-agent wins' is **not** a success criterion" (§8), "Limits are enforced in code, never only in prompts" (FR-11), and "Exactly-once LLM billing or tool execution is never claimed" (NFR-2). The open questions in §9 are genuinely open; none of them is rhetorical. Trade-offs are mostly implied, not named. §6 lists "No per-LLM-call checkpointing (possible v2)" but never says what that costs (a whole specialist invocation is re-billed after a crash). The PRD has no `[NOTE FOR PM]` callouts.

The real gap is that two decisions the thesis depends on are deferred or left undefined. The core comparison rests on "a comparable total budget" (FR-13, FR-23), and the PRD never says what "comparable" means: equal cost ceiling, equal token cap, or equal tool-call budget. OQ-8 ("How should precision and recall share one matching rule…") is filed as an open question, but without an answer SM-1's two main numbers are undefined. Both are product decisions, not architecture details.

### Findings
- **high** "Comparable total budget" is undefined (§4.4 FR-13, §4.8 FR-23, §1 Vision "for comparable cost") — The whole thesis compares the two modes "for comparable cost", yet the PRD never says which budget dimension is held equal. A multi-agent run that splits a cost ceiling across Planner and specialists behaves very differently from one held to the single agent's token cap. *Fix:* State the equalized quantity, e.g. "both modes run under the same estimated-cost ceiling and the same total tool-call cap; delegation limits apply only to multi-agent".
- **high** The metric matching rule is deferred although SM-1 depends on it (§9 OQ-8, §8 SM-1) — Recall and precision are the primary success measures, and their definition is an open question. *Fix:* Decide it in the PRD, e.g. "a finding matches a goldset defect when root cause matches semantically (operator judgment) and its location overlaps the seeded range; both precision and recall use this rule". Leave only the tooling for the architecture.
- **medium** Statistical limits of the experiment are not acknowledged (§4.8 FR-23, §8 SM-1) — 9 seeded defects × 3–5 trials can rarely separate the two modes with confidence. The PRD asks for "averages and variability" but never says that an inconclusive result is a legitimate outcome. *Fix:* Add one sentence: with n≤5 and 9 defects, the write-up reports the observed spread and may conclude "no detectable difference"; that still satisfies SM-1.

## Substance over theater — strong
Nothing here reads as furniture. There is a single persona (§2), and its three JTBDs map directly onto the three feature clusters: review, evaluation and durability. The Vision is specific to this product ("never claims more than it can prove", the multi-agent vs single question). It could not be swapped into another AI-review PRD. The NFRs are mostly concrete: NFR-1 names capabilities that must be absent, NFR-5 gives a dollar figure, and NFR-3 lists the reproducibility inputs. NFR-7 is the softest, but it still names the fields to trace. The PRD claims no differentiation. The addendum's landscape note keeps novelty claims modest ("a differentiator for a learning project").

### Findings
- **low** NFR-4 duplicates §2.2 and NFR-1 overlaps FR-7 (§5) — Some repetition, but each says it in testable terms. Acceptable at this size; no action needed unless trimming for length.

## Strategic coherence — strong
The thesis is explicit (§1) and the features serve it. The single-agent baseline (FR-13) is raised to a first-class mode *because* the comparison needs it, and the assumption tag says so honestly. The evaluation harness, cost accounting (FR-19) and evidence gate (FR-14) all serve "compare honestly". The success metrics validate the thesis, not activity: SM-1 publishes the comparison including losses. The counter-metrics are well chosen. SM-C1 (finding count) and SM-C3 (delegation count) block the obvious ways to game a multi-agent result. The secondary thesis (durability learning) has its own metric (SM-2). The MVP is a problem-solving/learning scope with matching logic.

The one gap: §7 puts "everything in §4" in scope and hands the ordering to `docs/implementation-plan.md`. The PRD gives no fallback if the ADR-002 model spike (OQ-1) fails. If the model can't do structured Planner output, the multi-agent half of the thesis cannot be built.

### Findings
- **medium** No fallback scope if the model spike fails (§7, §9 OQ-1) — OQ-1 can block the whole thesis, but §7 offers no reduced MVP. *Fix:* Add one line to §7: if OQ-1 fails, either swap the model (and re-baseline NFR-5) or ship single-agent plus durability only, with the comparison deferred.

## Done-ness clarity — adequate
Most FRs have testable consequences, and several are excellent. FR-14 ("fabricated or shifted line reference is marked `INVALID_EVIDENCE` and is never shown"), FR-17 ("at most one repair attempt") and FR-22 ("9 seeded defects and 1 non-defect control") need no interpretation. But a set of adjectives and undefined bounds remains, and some of them sit on load-bearing requirements.

`coverage` appears in FR-3 (implicitly), FR-5, FR-18 and FR-23 and in the glossary entry for PARTIAL. It is never defined: is it files touched, diff hunks inspected, or categories reviewed? FR-5's key guarantee ("never reports 'no issues found' for a run that did not cover the whole diff") cannot be tested without that definition. `NEEDS_REVIEW` is listed as an evidence status (§3) but no FR says when a candidate receives it, and the addendum (B7) concedes the semantics are undefined.

### Findings
- **high** "Coverage" is undefined but drives terminal-state honesty (§3 PARTIAL, §4.1 FR-5, §4.6 FR-18, §4.8 FR-23) — FR-5's central guarantee depends on a term with no definition. *Fix:* Add a glossary entry, e.g. "Coverage: the set of changed files (and hunks) that at least one specialist read via a tool, reported as covered/total; 'whole diff' = all changed, non-binary, in-policy files".
- **high** `NEEDS_REVIEW` has no trigger (§3 Evidence status, §4.5 FR-14) — FR-14 defines only the `INVALID_EVIDENCE` path. Engineers can't tell when a candidate is `NEEDS_REVIEW` instead of invalid, or whether it shows as a finding. *Fix:* Add a bullet to FR-14, e.g. "A candidate whose location exists but whose snippet matches only approximately (or that cites no snippet) is `NEEDS_REVIEW` and is listed separately from verified findings."
- **medium** Unbounded adjectives in FRs (§4.1 FR-1 "clear error", FR-4 "within a bounded time", §4.3 FR-12 "bounded summary", §4.2 FR-8 "oversized content", "out-of-policy submodules", FR-7 "for example, a file read returns at most 200 lines") — Only the 200-line limit is concrete, and it is phrased as an example. The addendum (B14) admits most limits have no defaults. *Fix:* Give defaults or explicit placeholders (e.g. cancel ≤ 30 s or at the next step boundary; summary ≤ N tokens; file > 1 MB refused; submodules refused entirely in v1), or mark each as `[ASSUMPTION]` with a value.
- **medium** SM-2 tests a scenario no FR specifies (§8 SM-2 "rate limit") — Rate-limit behaviour appears only as OQ-2. No FR says what must happen (back off and retry? end PARTIAL?). *Fix:* Add a consequence to FR-11 or FR-17: "Provider rate limits are retried with backoff within the elapsed-time budget; exhaustion ends the run `PARTIAL` with reason `RATE_LIMITED`".
- **medium** FR-16 "same normalized issue and location" is not testable as written (§4.5 FR-16) — The normalization is unspecified, and "same issue" is the semantic judgment that §6 says stays with the operator. *Fix:* Define dedup mechanically (same file + overlapping line range + same category), and state that semantic duplicates are left to the operator.
- **low** FR-18 determinism versus run-specific data (§4.6 FR-18 "identical content for identical inputs") — Reports contain latency, usage and retries, which differ between runs. The guarantee only holds if "inputs" includes the recorded usage. *Fix:* Say "identical content for the same recorded run state".
- **low** FR-3 has no consequence bullet (§4.1 FR-3) — *Fix:* e.g. "Status reflects a completed delegation within one step of it finishing."

## Scope honesty — strong
§6 Non-Goals does real work. It names things a reader would plausibly assume, such as auto-fix, PR comments, base-side citations and "No claim that a finding is a real bug". §7 lists deferrals (transcript debug artifact, object storage, large-repo indexing). The six `[ASSUMPTION]` tags are honest: each says where the PRD went beyond the source docs (cost limit added, rejected-candidates section added, conflict semantics invented). Open-items density (8 OQs + 6 assumptions) is fine for a hobby PoC. Two items, OQ-1 and OQ-8, are gating rather than incidental and are covered above.

### Findings
- **medium** The "explicitly approved" code path contradicts the non-goals (§2.2, §5 NFR-4 vs §6 "No remote, proprietary or bank repositories" and addendum B16) — The PRD body keeps the door open ("Only synthetic or explicitly approved code"). The addendum says v1 is synthetic-only and no approval path exists. *Fix:* In §2.2 and NFR-4, say "v1 sends only synthetic fixture code; an approval path for other code is `[NON-GOAL for MVP]`".
- **low** No `[NOTE FOR PM]` callouts (whole document) — The tensions are carried by OQs and assumptions instead, which is acceptable for a solo PRD. Optional: flag the budget-equality and matching-rule decisions this way.

## Downstream usability — adequate
The PRD feeds architecture and epics (the addendum is explicitly "for architecture and epics"), so this dimension matters. IDs are clean: FR-1..24, NFR-1..7, SM-1..5 and SM-C1..C3 are contiguous and unique, and each SM lists the FRs it validates. The glossary exists and covers the main nouns. The gaps are terms used as if defined but missing from the glossary, plus one outright contradiction in state semantics.

### Findings
- **high** The PARTIAL definition contradicts the CANCELLED and FAILED states (§3 PARTIAL "stopped early (budget, failure, cancellation)" vs §4.1 lifecycle and FR-4 "the run ends `CANCELLED`") — The glossary folds failure and cancellation into PARTIAL, while the lifecycle makes them distinct terminal states. Stories and the state machine will diverge. *Fix:* Redefine PARTIAL as "stopped early because a budget was exhausted or a non-fatal step failed, with usable findings". Define FAILED (no usable report) and CANCELLED (operator-initiated) separately.
- **medium** Report behaviour for FAILED/CANCELLED runs is unspecified (§4.6 FR-18 "Each finished run (COMPLETED or PARTIAL)" vs §4.1 FR-5) — FR-5 says these runs carry reasons and coverage, but FR-18 gives them no report. Where are findings gathered before cancellation kept? *Fix:* State whether CANCELLED and FAILED runs produce a report (or status-only reasons) and whether findings so far are preserved.
- **medium** Undefined domain terms (§4.3 FR-9 "topic", FR-11 "invocations per topic"; "delegation", "invocation", "attempt" in FR-3/FR-19/NFR-7; "manifest" FR-18; "fixture" FR-22/NFR-3) — "Topic" is a budget dimension with no definition. *Fix:* Add glossary entries for Topic, Delegation, Invocation, Attempt, Coverage, Manifest and Fixture.
- **low** Whether the Planner may choose Single is unresolved (§4.3 FR-9, §4.4 FR-13; addendum B5) — FR-9 rejects "unknown role" but never says whether Single is a known role in multi-agent mode. *Fix:* Add to FR-9: "In multi-agent mode the Planner may delegate only to Correctness, Security or Performance."

## Shape fit — strong
This is correctly shaped as a capability spec for a single-operator hobby tool. User journeys are omitted on purpose, JTBDs carry the motivation, and the SMs are operational, which is right for this shape. The length (~230 lines) suits the ~2–3 page target. Pushing tech detail (Temporal, LangChain4j, DeepSeek) into the addendum keeps §4 product-level. Small leaks remain in the PRD body ("durable orchestrator", "Activities" in the addendum only, the "worker process" in FR-20), and they are appropriate given the durability-learning JTBD. The PRD is neither over- nor under-formalized.
